Is school admission really free?
Yes! M.G. Oriental School offers 100% free admission for the academic year 2026-27. Only monthly tuition fees apply.
What is the crash course fee?
The crash course starts from 10th November at just ₹1000 (originally ₹1250). You get 20% discount if you join now.
Which classes are available in coaching?
New Era Coaching Centre offers classes from 1 to 12 (Hindi & English medium) for all subjects including Maths, Science, Social Science, English and Sanskrit.

School & coaching hours are Monday to Saturday, 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Contact us for specific class timings.
